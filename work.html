Work

  1. Erkki Kurenniemi: Pioneer of Digital Synthesis — Life, Work and Thoughts on Eternity is a collection of texts and essays on the Finnish scientist and composer Erkki Kurenniemi, a forgotten pioneer of electronic music.The book follows the life of a man who recorded himself obsessively for decades and who, long before us, asked the question we now put to our machines: if we keep enough of ourselves, can we survive our own death?

  2. Full Frames brings together a collection of photographs taken by Diogo Saraiva, between 2022 and 2025, with no set theme, chronology, or intention. This is just the instinctive gesture of capturing the moment, whether with a digital camera or a cell phone always at hand.Each image fills the entire page, without margins to contain it, inviting the reader to fully immerse themselves in each photographed moment. More than a thematic project, this book is a personal and fragmented archive of the gaze. A collection of isolated moments that, together, form an indirect portrait of three years of life experienced through the lens.

  3. Comédia (A)Normal, season 1, is a weekly series of stand-up comedy nights at Escola Normal – Casa Cultural, in Porto, promoted by Comédia a Preto & Branco.

  4. Paragem da Comédia is a series of stand-up comedy nights at Bus Paragem Cultural, in Anjos, Lisbon, promoted by Comédia a Preto & Branco and Porra Que Produções. Each edition has an original illustration and a colour of its own.

  5. Collaborators: Marco da Silva

    LoSaint Barberclub is a barbershop located in Valongo, Porto.More than a traditional barbershop, the space extends its offering beyond grooming services, also featuring a curated selection of fragrances and jewelry. It aims to offer the idea of community and belonging, aiming to provide the best possible experience for its customers, who are welcomed and treated as part of a large family.

  6. Collaborators: Marco da Silva

    RUGA is a Portuguese visual artist, based in Porto. His work is mainly done by the exploration of rug tufting and acrylic painting, with projects inspired by pop culture and personal experiences.

  7. Collaborators: Marco da Silva

    Uncrowded is a magazine that captures the essence of surf culture through the lens of Bernardo Guedes.Its pages document and bring together the places, the sessions, the road trips, the boards, the faces and the nights spent with friends.Between day and night, sea and land, the images form a personal diary of a way of life where the ocean sets the rhythm.

  8. Collaborators: Marco da Silva

    Portela d’Arcada is a rural home located in Fonte Arcada, Penafiel. Set in a quiet, nature-rich area, it embodies the calm and simplicity of life in the countryside. Surrounded by hills, stone paths, and seasonal landscapes, it’s a place shaped by its connection to the land and the slow rhythm of summer days.

  9. Collaborators: Marco da Silva, Nuno Pinto

    The Comédia a Preto & Branco project, developed by comedians Tomás Matos and David Santos, aims to promote stand-up comedy events and create humorous audiovisual content.This project brings together Portuguese comedians, providing them with an opportunity to take part in original productions and live shows.Comédia a Preto & Branco serves as a platform for interaction, promotion and participation in comedy, thereby helping to boost comedy events and bring together different generations of comedians on the national scene.

  10. Villa Regio is a short-term rental on Rua de Santo Amaro, near the historic centre of Vila do Conde, a town at the mouth of the river Ave that José Régio described as “spread out between pine forests, river and sea”.The identity is based on stencil lettering, whose bridges break up each letter as if it had been cut out.

  11. Collaborators: Alexandre Campelo

    Project Conta B or PCB is an accounting firm based on the outskirts of the city of Porto, and since 2007, it has focused its accounting work on the areas of taxation, management, and IEFP projects.

  12. Quase Irresponsável is a photographic project by Bernardo Guedes about a collection of photographs captured in the photographer’s night outings. An 80-page photo book archiving digital photographs taken between 2022-2023.

  13. This publication arises from the need to catalogue and reflect thoughts on appropriation in the art world and more specifically in graphic design, making an almost chronological and conceptual follow-up, presenting several texts, projects and examples of how appropriation has become a common and almost natural tool to the human being with all its pros and cons.Core of Pictus aims to catalogue the most important concepts present in the chosen texts of the readers and to tell the narrative of these words in image form.By assigning visual symbols or elements to individual letters, this project allows us to convey complex ideas associated with appropriation in a succinct and visually compelling manner, conveying the nuanced ideas and dynamics associated with appropriation in the realm of arts and design.

  14. Paris (dis)Order is a physical archive of a short trip to Paris by Bernardo Guedes.This publication with a photographic content shows us a set of images captured in the French capital during a short period of time. It gathers a selection of precise and simple images that together form this body of work, also precise and simple influenced by the city and its narratives.

  15. Collaborators: Hélder Pinho, José Pedro Carmo, Marco da Silva

    Past-Script is a case study that explores the enduring mystery surrounding a set of artefacts discovered in 1894 in the Serra do Alvão by priests José Brenha and Rafael Rodrigues. Motivated by local legends, the priests excavated ancient dolmens in search of treasures and uncovered a remarkable collection of stones and war objects engraved with anthropomorphic figures and symbols.Their findings gave rise to the intriguing theory that these engravings might represent one of the earliest forms of alphabetic writing in the world.
